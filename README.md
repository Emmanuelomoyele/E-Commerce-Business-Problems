                            

                                               EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Finance Director has noticed that revenue continues to grow every quarter, but profit has not grown at the same rate.  They are concerned that the business may be discounting too aggressively or that some product categories are less profitable than others.  Tomorrow morning, they have an executive meeting and ask: "I need a report that helps me identify which product categories are generating revenue but not necessarily generating profit. I want enough information to decide where we should investigate first.

                                              Headline Findings
1.Revenue and profitability are not evenly distributed across product categories. Some categories contribute a larger share of revenue than they do of profit, indicating differences in profitability.

2.Product categories with high revenue but low profit margins should be prioritised for further investigation. Strong sales performance does not necessarily translate into strong financial returns.

3.Comparing revenue share with profit share highlights potential inefficiencies. Categories whose revenue contribution significantly exceeds their 4.profit contribution may be affected by pricing, discounting, shipping costs, or other operational factors.

5.Profit margin varies across product categories. Categories with lower profit margins present opportunities to improve overall business profitability through pricing or cost optimisation.

                                              Recommendations
1.	Investigate categories with high revenue but low profit margins.
Review pricing strategy, supplier costs, discount policies, and operational expenses to identify the causes of reduced profitability.

2.	Review discount and promotional strategies.
Use the Discount_Percent, Discount_Amount, and Coupon_Used fields to determine whether excessive discounting is eroding profit.

3.	Analyse operational costs by category.
Assess whether high shipping costs, taxes, or fulfilment expenses are disproportionately affecting certain product categories.

4.	Prioritise high-margin categories for future investment.
Consider allocating more marketing budget and inventory to categories that consistently generate both strong revenue and strong profit margins.

5.	Perform a root-cause analysis on underperforming categories

                                           Extend the analysis by examining:
o	Average Discount Percentage 

o	Average Shipping Cost 

o	Coupon Usage Rate 

o	Quantity Sold 

o	Profit per Unit 

This will help determine whether low profitability is driven by pricing decisions, operational costs, or customer purchasing behaviour.
